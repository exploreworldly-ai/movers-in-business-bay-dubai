Prepare items with care

Professional Packing and Furniture Protection

The way belongings are packed affects how easily they can be lifted, loaded and organized at the destination. Select boxes and protective materials according to each item's size, weight and fragility.

01

Wrap fragile belongings

Use suitable cushioning for glassware, mirrors, decorations and other delicate items. Mark boxes clearly so their contents are easy to identify.

02

Prepare furniture

Check whether large beds, wardrobes or desks need dismantling to pass through doors, corridors or elevators. Keep fittings in labeled bags.

03

Organize boxes by room

Pack similar items together and label each box with its destination room. Keep heavy items in manageable containers.

04

Prepare electronics and essentials

Follow manufacturers' instructions, secure cables and back up important computer files. Keep keys, documents and daily essentials accessible.

If you have artwork, delicate furniture or unusually large belongings, mention them when discussing the moving requirements. Confirm which packing materials and tasks are included in the service scope.
